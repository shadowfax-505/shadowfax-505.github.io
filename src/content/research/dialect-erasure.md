---
title: Dialect erasure in AI transcription
area: Language technology · Bangla
question: When vision-language models read printed Sylheti or Chittagonian, do they quietly "correct" dialect words into Standard Bangla — and what makes them do it?
method: Controlled rendered-text test sets with matched dialect and standard items, several open models, and an inference-time fix evaluated on held-out items.
status: manuscript
order: 1
---
Transcription tools are increasingly used to digitise regional writing. If a model replaces a dialect word with its standard equivalent, the output reads fluently, and the dialect quietly disappears from the record. This project measures how often that happens, which conditions make it more likely, and whether a lightweight correction can reduce it without harming standard text.

Next steps are validation by native speakers and tests on real printed pages.
