---
title: Inventory service levels under stockout censoring
area: Operations research · Statistics
question: When a shop runs out of stock, its sales understate demand on exactly the days demand was high. Can a retailer still certify how often it meets demand?
method: An application of anytime-valid, randomisation-based inference (Waudby-Smith et al., 2024) to inventory decisions, evaluated on public retail datasets and in simulation.
status: manuscript
order: 4
---
Most inventory tools are tuned on sales, but sales stop at the shelf. The project studies what a retailer can guarantee about its service level if it randomises its own stock decisions slightly and logs how it did so, and what that costs in practice.

An independent check of the proofs and a field pilot with a retailer are the next steps.
