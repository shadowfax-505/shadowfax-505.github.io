## What it did

A gas sensor fed an ESP32. When it detected gas, the board sounded an alarm, switched on a fan and sent an alert over the web, so someone outside the room would know.

## What it taught me

It was my first complete loop: sense something physical, decide, act on it, and tell someone. Everything I have built since is a bigger version of that loop. I did not keep photos or the exact sensor part number, so this page describes the build rather than documenting it.
