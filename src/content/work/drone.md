## The build

In school, five of us spent about a year and a half building a quadcopter: frame, motors, flight controller, programming and GPS. The team was led by Sajib Mahmud Labim and Samrat Rahman; I was one of the three other members. The goal was a drone that could gather information during natural disasters.

## The flight

We showed it at the 40th National Science and Technology Week in Jhenaidah in March–April 2019. On the last day the Deputy Commissioner, Saroj Kumar Nath, flew it himself. Dainik Shiksha reported the project on 1 April 2019.

## About the model on this page

I no longer have photos of the drone. The 3D model is a reconstruction of an F450-class quadcopter with a GPS mast, the same class of frame we used. It shows the kind of machine we built; it is not a scan of it.
